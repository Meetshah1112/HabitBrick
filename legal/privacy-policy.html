HabitBrick · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

The short version: HabitBrick does not have accounts, does not use the internet, and does not send your data anywhere. Every habit, streak, badge, and setting you create is stored only on your own device. We don't have servers, so we literally cannot see your data.

1. Information We Collect

HabitBrick does not require you to create an account, and does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

  • Name, email address, or password
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or files
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers
  • Analytics or usage data sent to us or any third party

The only information HabitBrick stores is what you enter directly into the app — habit names, categories, completion history, streaks, badges earned, reminder times, and the display name you optionally set on your profile. This information is saved using your device's local storage (AsyncStorage) and never leaves your device.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Everything you enter is used solely to run the app on your device: calculating streaks, displaying your consistency graph, unlocking badges, and showing your progress in the Brick World screen. Because this data is never transmitted, there is no server-side processing, no data sale, and no sharing with advertisers or data brokers of any kind.

3. Notifications

If you enable habit reminders, HabitBrick schedules local notifications directly on your device using your operating system's notification system. These reminders (including the alarm-based nags and the end-of-day "last chance" reminder) are generated and delivered entirely on-device — no reminder content or scheduling data is sent to us or any external service.

4. Sharing Badge Achievements

If you choose to share an unlocked badge, HabitBrick generates an image and hands it to your device's native share sheet so you can send it via whichever app you pick (e.g. Messages, WhatsApp, Instagram). This action is entirely user-initiated. HabitBrick does not collect, store, or have visibility into what you share or where you send it.

5. Third-Party Services

HabitBrick does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or cloud-storage SDKs. The app makes no network requests of any kind during normal use.

6. Data Storage and Security

All app data is stored locally on your device using standard secure on-device storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. Because no data is transmitted or stored on any server we operate, there is no remote database that could be breached.

7. Data Deletion

You are always in full control of your data:

  • Reset Progress: Open the profile menu from the Home screen and select "Reset Progress" to permanently erase all habits, streaks, and badges from your device.
  • Uninstall the app: Uninstalling HabitBrick permanently deletes all locally stored data, since nothing exists outside your device.

Because we never receive your data in the first place, there is no separate deletion request process needed — deleting it on-device is complete and final.

8. Children's Privacy

HabitBrick is a general-audience habit-tracking app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from any user at all.

9. Permissions

HabitBrick requests the following device permissions, used only for the stated purpose:

  • Notifications / Schedule Exact Alarm: to deliver the habit reminders you configure, at the times you choose.
  • Receive Boot Completed: to re-register your scheduled reminders after your device restarts.
  • Vibrate: to accompany reminder notifications with vibration feedback.

No other device permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, etc.) are requested.

10. Changes to This Policy

If HabitBrick's data practices change in the future (for example, if an optional account or backup feature is introduced), this policy will be updated first, with a revised "Last updated" date, before that feature ships.
